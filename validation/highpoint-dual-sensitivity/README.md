# Private full-component N6 sensitivity: portable evidence

This capsule preserves a private full-Taylor sensitivity prototype, its actual
19 selected passing tests, and one completed four-domain numerical correctness
cohort. It does not adopt the prototype in production or expose public highpoint
Dual interfaces. A target-only draft remains pending until an independent saved
numerical audit and a separate root/parent audit-clear permit sealing.

The retained numerical point is a massive scalar N6 fixture with unit powers,
rank zero, zero dimension shift and requested accuracy of 53 bits. Float128 and
Float256 each exercise two components for an independent first squared-mass
derivative and six components for a coherent physical dilation. The first mass
derivative is checked against independently reduced dotted input and original
IREGI. Dilation coefficients come from Symbolica. These are two different
derivative checks; they do not qualify general higher or mixed independent mass
and shift derivatives, or finite differences.

Every native UV single, IR single, IR double and finite coefficient remains in
the raw captures. Complete parent and child sensitivity operands, raw and

## Portable checking and reconstruction

After sealing, run from the repository root using Python's standard library:

```sh
python3 validation/highpoint-dual-sensitivity/check.py
python3 validation/highpoint-dual-sensitivity/check.py --reconstruct target/highpoint-dual-restored
```

A target-only draft is checked with its own `check.py --allow-draft`; that flag
cannot admit an unsealed directory under `validation`. The checker safely reads
regular archive members and verifies exact capsule/borrowed inventories. It
inherits the committed `exact-master-variant-cache` capsule and reconstructs its
88-file candidate. Each historical component patch independently rebuilds its
own 89-file source version. The final prototype plus each capture patch rebuilds
the corresponding complete 92-file numerical source, and three committed
fixtures reconstruct the 92-file synthetic test build overlay. Large generated
master programs are inherited, not duplicated.

The portable saved-value checker consumes supplied byte maps only. It imports
no producer admission validator, reads no original ignored target path, and
opens no native executable, library or omitted build dependency. It recomputes
all retained fixed-input, target, context, batching and full precision-pair
checks from saved rational numbers and compares them to the unchanged saved
gate. All original source findings and repairs remain visible, including the
rejected first numerical runner status gate and its corrected revision.

The committed parent also preserves the earlier same-budget 180-second typed
cache pilot: f64 stability rejection, a successful DoubleFloat point, partial
Float128/256 schedules and the original driver link failure. Completing this
new cohort under predeclared larger correctness budgets does not alter those
historical failures, establish a cache speedup, or support a timing comparison.

## Provenance limits

Nine newly produced compiled-product instances and generated Python bytecode
are omitted explicitly. Original artifact digests, commands, finite bounds,
grants, logs, source/native input maps and saved terminal process-group records
remain. Compiled-product digest chains are checked as historical producer
evidence, without rehashing omitted products. Only reconstructed or committed
guard bytes are rechecked; unbundled native/tool/OS guard occurrences are counted
and remain historical. No live historical process identifier is probed.

No compiler, evaluator, scalar or tensor integral, reference execution, benchmark
or profiler runs during packaging or portable checking. Current Cargo/full-suite
qualification, public highpoint Dual support, general derivative coverage,
rigorous backend uncertainty, and performance parity remain open. Separate
unexecuted rectangular-jet work is excluded from this qualified cohort.

`package.py` is the deterministic one-time packaging recipe. It requires an
explicit independent-audit-clear before writing the final validation directory,
refuses in-place resealing, and records all exclusions. The raw qualification
summary retains its original pending-audit wording; the final independent audit
and capsule seal are separate later evidence and do not rewrite that history.
