# Existing Symbolica DAG arithmetic for high-point guard research

Status: source-only design, not implemented, compiled, timed, or adopted. The public guard policy and diagnostics stay unchanged. This addresses the observed expensive primal-amplitude preparation; it does not establish a speedup or replace scalar-master validation.

## Finding and scope

The existing exact reduction evaluator can be mapped to Symbolica RealBall/ComplexBall and evaluated in batches without constructing a second expanded stability polynomial. This is a viable backend for an *opt-in conditional recurrence-roundoff check*. It is not a drop-in replacement for the current conditioning estimate, and not a certificate of the whole integral.

`StabilityPlan::prepare_primal_amplitudes` currently walks reachable aliases and calls `master_amplitude`. The latter uses Symbolica `to_polynomial_in_vars_with_field` to extract affine dependence on immediate master-dependent aliases, recursively constructs lazy conditional amplitudes, and emits positive sum roots. Re-evaluating the original moment/alias DAG would avoid that particular polynomial extraction and amplitude-root construction. It would not remove existing plan construction, alias lowering, raw-distance certificates, master activation, or local-monitor preparation unless separately redesigned. In particular, compiling the current already-expanded monitor roots into balls would preserve the preparation bottleneck.

## Three reuse checks

1. Symbolica implementation: `src/evaluate/domain.rs` exposes EvaluationDomain, which chooses numeric function implementations; it does not itself propagate errors. `ExpressionEvaluator::map_coeff_with_prec` in evaluator.rs preserves the instruction graph while mapping exact coefficients. `export_instructions` in instruction.rs exposes Add/Mul/integerPow/Powf/functions/Assign/IfElse/Goto/Join and nested shared evaluators. Existing RealBall/ComplexBall domains and directed rational arithmetic are implemented in numerica float/interval.rs. No independent polynomial or arithmetic engine is needed.
2. Symbolica usage/tests: `tests/evaluation.rs::error_propagating_float_transcendental_evaluator` demonstrates numeric-domain mapping, not an enclosure theorem. domain.rs ball_tests and interval.rs tests cover rational enclosures, invalid denominators and nonnegative real square roots. BatchExpressionEvaluator retains instruction-major execution, shared callees and lazy per-lane branches. Dualizer::vectorize already flattens ordered full Taylor shapes and shared functions before coefficient mapping.
3. FeynKit/gammaloop/spenso: searches for EvaluationDomain, ErrorPropagatingFloat, RealBall, ComplexBall, interval/error propagation find Symbolica wrapper implementations (gammalooprs/utils and spenso network/tensor mapping), but no reusable conservative branch-aware DAG guard. Existing momentum precision and Newton residual checks are different contracts. This is a search result, not a claim that no such facility can ever exist.

## Existing types and why the tempting shortcut is unsuitable

RealBall::from_rational_ball and ComplexBall::from_rational_ball enclose exact represented Rational inputs with explicitly specified radii. Addition/subtraction/multiplication/division/inverse and nonnegative integer powers use certified arithmetic; real sqrt is certifying only for finite nonnegative balls. Complex transcendental methods and inherited log/exp/etc are explicitly not certifying. Unknown constants/functions must not silently fall back to rounded Float callbacks. `map_coeff_with_prec` resolves constant-function slots as well as literal coefficients; export and inspect those slots before mapping.

ErrorPropagatingFloat stores `abs_err: f64`, converts operands with `to_f64`, and implements linearized propagation. Multiplication omits the general product-of-errors term, inversion uses a first-order derivative, and primitive addition does not establish an outward-rounded interval. It therefore does not provide a conservative arbitrary-precision certificate. It may be a separately named diagnostic estimate, but cannot silently replace this project's range/mixed-precision guards.

## Two distinct claims

*Represented-input recurrence check*: Treat each original routing/Taylor coefficient and each returned master Laurent/Taylor coefficient as its exact represented value. Evaluate the exact recurrence DAG with enclosing coefficients/inputs. Compare each actual returned coefficient, converted exactly, against the resulting enclosure: the maximum possible difference is bounded by outward-rounded endpoint differences. The parameter boundary must be explicit: if the exported DAG takes already-rounded derived distances or normalization factors, the bound starts after that preprocessing. Feeding the exact original-coordinate DAG would include it; otherwise the existing derived-input guard remains necessary. This measures arithmetic error in recombining those specific represented values. It says nothing about the difference between a returned master and the mathematical scalar integral, or about incorrect analytic branch selection. Radius alone is not the error of the production result unless its center equals that result; compare against both enclosing endpoints.

*Conditional uncertainty propagation*: To replace the current compounded-master sensitivity estimate, assign master-input radii covering the same declared uncertainty assumptions, and propagate those through the original DAG. The resulting enclosure is mathematically conditional on those radii. Existing operand-roundoff estimates are not certified master-error bounds, so the final diagnostic must continue to state estimate/assumption rather than certificate of the integral. Repeated occurrences lose correlation and can enlarge intervals, causing honest rejection. Exact zeros must not erase uncertain inputs: retain the current active-master original-scale/Taylor provenance gate and scaleless exemption. The restricted new near-vacuum D0 facility supplies actual finite enclosures only for its explicit supported domain; it does not cover all A0/B0/C0/D0 Laurent coefficients.

These two policies cannot be conflated. A zero-radius represented-master check can miss large errors already present in masters which the existing amplitude estimate was intended to flag.

## Branch blocker and narrow initial support

The generic evaluator executes IF by `is_fully_zero`. RealBall returns false for an interval containing zero with nonzero radius, so such an uncertain condition would be taken as true. PartialOrd compares centers, not separated interval bounds. Consequently mapping an arbitrary conditional recurrence to balls is unsafe without additional control-flow validation. A numeric zero/equality comparison or center-based decision is not a certificate.

The smallest viable experiment accepts only branch-free exact rational arithmetic graphs with add/mul/negation/conjugation/integer powers and denominator enclosures excluding zero; retain scalar masters as input parameters. Reject Powf, registered nonrational constant functions, unresolved external callbacks and all uncertified transcendental operations. Start with ComplexBall for every lane; real-only grouping can reuse the reviewed all-argument/all-Taylor classifier later. Full Dual support uses existing Symbolica Dualizer before whitelisting, so every resulting Taylor operation must pass the same whitelist. Fixed negative integer exponents require denominator enclosures excluding zero; any noninteger exponent remains unsupported. Unsupported or nonfinite callbacks fail closed per lane even if coefficient mapping succeeded. Zero-radius exact inputs may acquire enclosure radius during finite-precision conversion and arithmetic; a zero center alone proves nothing.

A later extension may accept reachable IF/comparison nodes only when an independent, context-bound exact primal decision has already been proved, or when both interval alternatives can be safely enclosed. The existing raw-distance Zero receipt proves only its exact ordered polynomial/Taylor masks and does not authorize arbitrary Cayley/Gram/predicate decisions. The evaluator has no fallible ambiguous-branch hook. Reusing it without silent choices therefore needs either Symbolica-based specialization using proved branch decisions or a narrowly reviewed Symbolica evaluator API extension. Do not write a second handwritten interpreter. Proving all predicates eagerly is also wrong if it evaluates singular inactive branches. Any branch extension must preserve lazy control-flow semantics and lane failure isolation.

## Minimal private experiment and acceptance

1. Compile only the original recurrence roots and aliases using the existing exact evaluator builder. Retain exact coefficients, full ordered Dual shape, input policy and mass dimensions. Traverse the exported instructions and shared bodies once for the operation whitelist; do not expand expressions or manipulate polynomial coefficients. Record root/alias/instruction/temporary counts and preparation time separately.
2. Preserve the current ordinary production evaluation, input validation, derived-invariant certificates, requested bits and scale-provenance checks. Initially run the new bound side-by-side as a diagnostic; it must not alter results or statuses. No automatic success when the old guard rejects. Unsupported or unresolved cases remain explicit in the experimental result.
3. Instantiate exact represented-input balls with an explicit precision budget. Batch only active lanes and refine only unresolved ones. Use existing outward arithmetic to compare the exact represented result with the full enclosing interval and the unchanged dimensional target. Include final convention/scale conversion if the claim concerns the returned coefficient; otherwise name the earlier boundary explicitly. A mathematically evaluated target also needs a conservative lower enclosure for acceptance, rather than rounding the allowed error upward. Reject invalid/zero-containing denominators, nonfinite or out-of-range targets and unsupported functions. Preserve original Taylor precision rather than using prototype precision to invent information.
4. For the conditional master-uncertainty version, first specify each radius and its provenance, and independently demonstrate that the policy reproduces or conservatively strengthens the intended current master-sensitivity assumptions. This is required before removing expanded amplitude construction. Do not call an assumption-based master bound a mathematical integral certificate.
5. Bound tests before a large compile: exact Rational branch-free oracles, adversarial cancellation, reciprocal near-zero and complex denominator cases, exact zero vs uncertainty, tiny/huge/subnormal inputs, Float mixed precision and DD low limbs, all first/second/mixed Taylor components, invalid/unstable lane compaction and repeated calls, and explicit unsupported branches/functions/constants. Existing rounded-zero and original-scale-jet witnesses must remain rejected.
6. Only after correctness compare high-point preparation/memory and warmed singleton/batch runtime. Ball arithmetic may greatly increase numeric cost and correlation overestimation; a preparation win alone is insufficient. Use independent reference/precision rescue fixtures and the existing99%paired interval/width gate. Keep diagnostics/failure precedence under a separately named policy until qualification supports any default change.

Practical next step: a source-only operation census of one bounded original high-point recurrence DAG, to determine whether branch-free support reaches meaningful work at all. This is more useful than immediately building a broad custom error domain. Current default policy remains unchanged.
